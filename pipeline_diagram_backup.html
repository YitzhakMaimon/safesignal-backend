SAFE//SIGNAL

SafeSignal listens to social media in real time, detecting signs of distress and risk within an ocean of content, and routes them through a chain of analysis, context retrieval, and decision-making — until a trustworthy alert arrives in time.

10 STAGES 3 INGEST SOURCES 2× GUARDRAILS REAL-TIME OUTPUT
  1. 00
    STEP 00 · INGEST

    Signal Ingestion

    The system monitors Telegram, Reddit, and Twitter/X channels in real time, capturing every suspicious message the moment it's posted — before it's lost in the stream.

    TelegramRedditTwitter / X
  2. 01
    STEP 01 · STORE

    Raw Archival in S3

    Every captured message is stored exactly as received, unprocessed, in a dedicated bucket — a source copy for auditing, recovery, and future model training.

    Amazon S3
  3. 02
    STEP 02 · NLP

    Language Understanding

    HeBERT reads Hebrew in all its nuance — slang, spelling errors, and cultural context — while Comprehend, in parallel, detects entities, sentiment, and personal information, with support for 12 languages.

    HeBERTAWS Comprehend12 Languages
  4. 03
    STEP 03 · INFERENCE

    Local Inference Engine — Ollama

    A local language model named Ollama runs inside the infrastructure itself, keeping personal details and identifiers private.

    Ollama · Local Model
  5. 04
    STEP 04 · CLASSIFY

    Distress Classification (Bedrock)

    Incoming content is classified, then passed down the chain to the decision-agent node.

    Bedrock
  6. 05
    STEP 05 · RETRIEVE

    Context Retrieval (RAG)

    Before a decision is made, the system retrieves relevant documents, protocols, and precedents from a dedicated knowledge base — grounding the decision in facts rather than the model's guess alone.

    Vector StoreRAG
  7. 06
    STEP 06 · DECIDE

    Decision Agent

    A LangGraph-based agent graph combines every insight gathered so far, weighs the severity of the evidence, and decides: dismiss, monitor, or alert.

    LangGraph
  8. 07
    STEP 07 · SCREEN

    Final Output Screening

    Before the alert goes out, a Guardrails layer filters unnecessary identifying information — but deliberately preserves contact details for crisis and emergency hotlines.

    Bedrock Guardrails
  9. 08
    STEP 08 · EVALUATE

    Reliability Evaluation (RAGAS)

    Before an alert is considered trustworthy, RAGAS checks how faithful the decision is to the retrieved sources, and how relevant it is to the original event.

    RAGAS
  10. 09
    STEP 09 · OUTPUT

    Real-Time Alert

    The final finding — with a risk level, explanation, and sources — reaches the SafeSignal dashboard and everyone who needs to act, in real time.

    SafeSignal Dashboard
